Founding Product Engineer at [Prom](https://prom.dev)

> Hi, this is a cult. Come in.

Prom is an AI-powered social platform for creating things with your friends, on iOS, Android, and the web. Your people form a Circle: one group chat for everyone. Delphi, Prom's AI, helps the Circle make Charms: apps, games, rituals, and interactive experiences that live inside the chat. Keep a tiny creature alive together, turn your group chat into a strategy game, or build a birthday ritual out of ten years of incriminating photos.

There's no feed and no audience. Everyone in a Circle is a member, and membership means making things together.
