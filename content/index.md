I build zero-to-one products because, selfishly, I want to see cool things exist. Right now that’s [[Prom]], where I’m the founding product engineer.

Prom is built for group chats to craft their own lore and rituals with AI. Keep a tiny creature alive together, turn the chat into a strategy game, or build a birthday ritual out of ten years of incriminating photos. No feed, no audience, just your people. Try it at [prom.dev](https://prom.dev).

I built this site with [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) (my favorite note-taking app) and [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) (a static site generator) to serve as my personal Wikipedia - a place to document my work, projects, and ideas.

Any highlighted word is clickable and takes you to another page (like a wiki). For example, 👉[[Socials|this]]👈 link goes to my socials and contact info.

Check out what I’m building at [[Prom]], some of my past [[Project README|projects]], see where I’ve [[Work README|worked]], or read some [[Quotes I Like|quotes I like]].
